Terms


Definitions

The following definitions are used but not otherwise defined in this Agreement:

  • “Casualty Value” means the market value of the Equipment at the end of the Term or, in relation to a Total Loss, the market value the Equipment would have had at the end of the Term but for the Total Loss. The Casualty Value may be less than but will not exceed the original purchase price of the Equipment.
  • “Equipment” means Skytrak 8042, which has an approximate value of $43,000.00.
  • “Total Loss” means any loss or damage that is not repairable or that would cost more to repair than the market value of the Equipment.
Lease

The Lessor agrees to lease the Equipment to the Lessee, and the Lessee agrees to lease the Equipment from the Lessor in accordance with the terms set out in the Agreement.

Term

The Agreement commences on and concludes on the dates specified in the corresponding invoice, unless additional rental agreements supersede.

Rent

Rent will be paid prior to the Lessee taking possession of the Equipment.

Delivery of Equipment

The Lessor will deliver the Equipment to the Lessee at the agreed locations specified in the corresponding invoice.

Use of Equipment

Use the equipment carefully and for its intended purpose. Comply with all manufacturer requirements/recommendations and applicable laws. Do not alter/modify the equipment without the owner’s permission. Equipment may not be moved from the specified job site or driven on the road any distance exceeding 1⁄2 mile.

Warranties
  • The Equipment will be in good working order and condition upon delivery.
  • The Equipment is of merchantable quality and is fit for the purposes it is ordinarily used.
Loss and Damage / Customer Responsibilities

To the extent permitted by law, the Lessee will be responsible for the risk of loss, theft, damage, or destruction to the Equipment from any cause. Includes tire, engine, chassis, seat control, hand control, and forklift carriage due to misuse or negligence.

Lessee is required to:

  • Perform regular fluid checks and top off as necessary, including coolant, fuel, hydraulic, engine, differential, and axle.
  • Maintain at least 1/4 tank fuel level (#2 Diesel only).
  • Repair or replace hydraulic hoses and fittings as needed.
  • Keep the machine clean and free of mud and debris.

Lessee is required to prevent:

  • Overloading of the starter motor.
  • Shock loading of driveline and transmission components.
  • Critical discharge of the battery.
  • Damage to the key switch.
  • Tire slipping resulting in chunking or tearing of tread.
  • Driving in mud or loose material beyond axle depth.
  • Contacting overhead power lines.
  • Machine tip-over.
  • Glass and vinyl cracking or breakage.
  • Damage to machine controls and switches.
Loss and Damage / Customer Responsibilities

If the Equipment is lost or damaged, the Lessee will continue paying Rent, and will provide the Lessor with prompt written notice of such loss or damage and will pay to the Lessor all unpaid Rent for the Term plus the Casualty Value of the Equipment. 10. In the event of Total Loss of the Equipment, the Lessee will provide the Lessor with prompt written notice of such loss and will pay to the Lessor all unpaid Rent for the Term plus the Casualty Value of the Equipment.

Ownership, Right to Lease and Quiet Enjoyment
  • The Equipment is the property of the Lessor and will remain the property of the Lessor.
  • The Lessee will not encumber the Equipment or pledge it as security.
  • The Lessee warrants the Lessor's right to lease the Equipment under this Agreement.
  • The Lessor warrants that, provided no Event of Default has occurred, they will not disturb the Lessee’s peaceful possession or use of the Equipment.
Surrender

At the end of the Term or upon earlier termination, the Lessee will make the Equipment available for pick-up within 100ft of the Equipment drop-off address.

Indemnity

The Lessee will indemnify and hold harmless the Lessor against claims, actions, costs, damages, and liabilities, including attorney’s fees, arising from the Lessee’s use of the Equipment.

Default

An Event of Default includes:

  • Failure to pay amounts due.
  • Becoming insolvent or filing for bankruptcy.
  • A writ of attachment on the Equipment not released within 10 days.
Remedies

On an Event of Default, the Lessor may:

  • Declare all Rent for the Term immediately due.
  • Commence legal proceedings for recovery.
  • Take possession of the Equipment.
  • Terminate the Agreement.
  • Pursue other legal or equitable remedies.
Assignment

The Lessee may not assign this Agreement or their interest in the Equipment without the Lessor's consent.

Additional Documents

The Lessee will execute and deliver documents required by the Lessor to protect the Lessor’s interest.

Entire Agreement

Governing Law
