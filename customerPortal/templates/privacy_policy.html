Privacy Policy

Welcome to ezRent (by Project Jumpstart, LLC). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information, and it will be the shortest privacy policy you ever read (or don't).

Your phone number or email will be used to send text messages or emails updating you on the status of your rental requests. We do not share your information with third parties unless required by law. We will never use your phone number for ANY PURPOSE other than to notify you about pertinent updates concerning your rental request.

Data Security

We take appropriate measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

Your Rights

You have the right to opt out of receiving text messages by replying STOP. You may also request that we delete your phone number from our records by contacting our support team.

We reserve the right to update this Privacy Policy. Changes will be communicated to you and posted on this page.
